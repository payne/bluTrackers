Who flew in: off-site detections across the Blu network

Tagged monarchs and bats picked up by Blu Series receivers in eastern Nebraska and western Iowa. Only animals tagged somewhere other than the detecting station are listed. Monarch tags are not reported to the Motus website, so these records were downloaded from each receiver and cross-referenced with Project Monarch.

    Field notes

    From the station operator's report.

    • Only off-site detections are reported. An animal tagged at a station and heard by that same station's receiver is left out.
    • Many monarchs were tagged at Ponca State Park, which produced a large volume of data. It was sorted by hand for outside visitors and none were found, though some could have been missed.
    • The eastern red bat and silver-haired bat heard at Ponca on Sep 13 will be posted on Motus.
    • The Parry Sound, Ontario monarch heard at Indian Cave State Park on Oct 2 is a different individual from the Parry Sound monarch heard at Peru State College on Sep 21.
    • Several monarchs passing between Blu sites were tagged locally at Zoo Omaha and at Glacier Creek Preserve (Dr. Bragg).
    • The same monarch can be heard by more than one receiver, so a detection count is not a count of individual animals.
